About me

I'm Ekbal Ahmed Sadik — most people just call me Sadik. I'm studying Computer Science & Engineering at Uttara University in Dhaka, and I've been working with computers professionally since 2021.

My path into engineering came from the practical side: managing records, documents and day-to-day computer operations for passion. That work taught me precision, patience, and how to keep systems tidy for the people who depend on them — habits that carry straight over into writing code.

Alongside that, I design. Years in Photoshop and Illustrator gave me an eye for layout, contrast and hierarchy, so I care as much about how an interface feels as about whether it compiles. Right now I'm deepening my foundations in C, data structures and web development.
